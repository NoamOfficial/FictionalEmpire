📜 Rise and Fall

1197 — Frederick de Potolamo acquires Slovenia and Friuli.
1202–1207 — Raymond I reigns as King of Friuli-Potolamo.
1207–1227 — Salzburg and Lower Hungary are conquered.
1228 — Battle of Friuli; Frederick I dies after 21 years on the throne.
1228–1229 — Austria conquers Lower Hungary.
1230 — Crusaders crush Frederick II and force him into German vassalage.
1231 — Antioch is occupied after the Battle of Damascus.
1232 — Cyrene and Cyprus are occupied.
1237 — Frederick II dies; Otto I the Victorious is crowned.
1239 — Athens and Thessaloniki are occupied.
1241 — Wallachia is occupied jure uxoris.
1243 — Cyprus revolts.
1245 — Cyprus is occupied again.
1251 — Otto I reverses the German vassalage.
1261 — Otto I dies; the Frederickian Dynasty becomes extinct.
1261–1263 — Unrouch the Brave reigns.
1262 — The Ottonian Dynasty begins.
1263 — Otto II of Cyprus deposes Unrouch.
1265 — The empire reaches its peak: Lower Hungary, Athens, Cyprus, Friuli, Slovenia and a small Antioch exclave.
1271 — Otto II leaves no legitimate heir; the empire collapses.
